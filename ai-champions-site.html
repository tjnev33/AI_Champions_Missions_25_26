AI Champions

Learners leading learners — helping our staff explore what AI makes possible, responsibly.

The biggest risk isn't AI disruption — it's believing that what we do now is all there is. Our job is to notice the unseen opportunities forming and help colleagues ask: "What new thing is this making possible in my classroom, my workflow, and student learning?"

The Roadmap

What Is an AI Champion?

Lead by example. Explore AI to enrich teaching and learning — aligned to our FPS AI Use Guide and shared principles.

🔬

Explore & Experiment

Try AI tools in low-stakes settings. Build fluency through small, practical challenges — not theory alone.

🧭

Model Responsible Use

Demonstrate what thoughtful AI integration looks like — including what not to do — so colleagues see the guardrails in action.

🤝

Lead & Lift Others

Grow from personal skill-building into instructional leadership — sharing resources, running micro-PDs, and supporting colleagues.

⭐

North Star — Ask This Every Week

"Does this deepen learning, strengthen student agency, and reduce busywork — without bypassing thinking?"

Built-in Flexibility

The suggested pace is just that — a suggestion. You have 12 full weeks to complete 10 missions. Need to double up one week and skip the next? Go for it. The buffer weeks are there so you never feel behind. What matters is finishing, not matching the calendar perfectly.

After the Missions
